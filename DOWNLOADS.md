# Downloads needed (Krishn)

## 1. Mixamo (animations, the big one)
Log into **mixamo.com** in the Mixamo tab Claude opened in Chrome (any Adobe account). Tell Claude when you are in; it fetches about 40 clips (swings, flips, landings, combos, dodges, hit reactions) through that tab.
