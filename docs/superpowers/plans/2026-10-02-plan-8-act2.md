# Plan 8: Act 2, "Power and Illusions" (Electro, Scorpion, Mysterio, Lizard) and Miles mission 1

**Goal:** Act 2 plays end to end on the Plan 7 framework: a Harbor blackout and Electro at the
power station, Miles holding the line at the shelter during the blackout, Scorpion on the
Queensway Bridge with a poison timer, Mysterio's illusion premiere in Neon Square ending in a
giant Mysterio, and the Lizard through the park and the zoo.

**Architecture:** Content on the existing director, plus four small framework additions:
- `step.char`: a step can put the player in another roster character (Miles), and the next step
  without one puts Peter back. The switch happens on a fade, never mid-fight.
- `defend` step type: wave fights with a protected object (a generator) that enemies attack;
  if it breaks, the step retries.
- A HUD timer (`ui.timer(label, seconds)`) for the poison fight.
- The illusion layer: a camera roll and a green ink tint while Mysterio's smoke is up (visual
  only; the physics rule holds), and the spider-sense scan (V) marking the real Mysterio among
  decoys.

**Global constraints:** as Plan 7 (no dashes in copy, step ids, physics rule, muted test browsers,
commits by Krishn Gohel only, no deploy).

## Bosses
- **Electro** (power station roof). Charged: blows on him shock you back and do nothing; webs
  fizzle (spec: electric web does nothing). Three relay boxes on the roof: web yank one and it
  shorts, draining him for a window. Attacks: an arc bolt along a line (buildings block it),
  a ground pulse up close, slow ball lightning. Phase 2 (50%): he rides the magnetic pull up a
  chimney and calls bolts down on marked circles; yank the chimney relay and he falls, drained.
- **Scorpion** (Queensway Bridge). Chase: he runs and vaults along the deck toward Queens, the
  hero catches him (three hits). Fight on the deck: a long tail sting that poisons (a timer
  starts: beat him before it runs out; perfect dodges buy time), acid spit, a wide tail sweep,
  a pounce from the bridge cables.
- **Mysterio** (Neon Square, night). Phase 1: four decoys and the real one; hits pop decoys,
  the spider-sense scan (V) marks the real one. Phase 2: the giant Mysterio (his model at 8x)
  looms over the plaza and slams down fists on telegraphed spots; three projector drones keep
  him there: yank or punch them down. Then the real Mysterio, short and dizzy.
- **Lizard** (Central Park). Chase: he runs and climbs across the park where there are no
  anchors (zip and run), three hits. Fight at the zoo: leaps, tail swipes, a roar that calls
  three lizard minions; in phase 3, web him down while stunned (six web hits) so Connors can be
  given the cure.
